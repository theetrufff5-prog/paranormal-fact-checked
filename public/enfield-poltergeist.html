CASE FILE / ENFIELD, LONDON

The Enfield Poltergeist

REPORTS DOCUMENTED / CAUSE UNRESOLVED

A heavily documented disturbance included numerous witnesses and recordings—but also admitted trickery and a much smaller Warren role than the movies suggest.

Assessment published September 2026

Verified factThe Hodgson family reported disturbances beginning in 1977, drawing police, journalists, and Society for Psychical Research investigators.
Complicating evidenceThe children were caught staging some incidents; investigators disagreed about whether that explained everything.
Film embellishmentThe Warrens did not lead or resolve the investigation as portrayed in The Conjuring 2.

The investigation

Peggy Hodgson and her children reported knocks, displaced furniture, thrown objects, apparitions, and a deep voice at their council house beginning in August 1977. Police officer Carolyn Heeps reported seeing a chair move, although that observation did not identify its cause.

Maurice Grosse and Guy Lyon Playfair of the Society for Psychical Research became the principal investigators. They collected testimony, photographs, and many hours of recordings. Some observers believed that part of the activity remained unexplained; others found evidence of misinterpretation or deliberate performance.

Trickery and uncertainty

Janet and Margaret Hodgson acknowledged faking some events, and observers caught or suspected them on multiple occasions. Supporters argued that the admitted trickery represented only a small portion of the total reports. Skeptics considered it a serious reliability problem, particularly because many incidents centered on Janet.

The surviving material documents a remarkable social and media event. It does not provide a controlled, reproducible demonstration of a poltergeist.

The Warrens’ actual role

The Warrens visited, but Playfair later rejected the suggestion that they conducted the central investigation. He described their involvement as brief. The film’s exorcistic confrontation, possession plot, and heroic resolution are dramatizations.

Assessment

The reports, investigation, recordings, and some deliberate trickery are documented. Whether every reported event was staged or misinterpreted cannot be conclusively reconstructed. No supernatural cause has been established.
