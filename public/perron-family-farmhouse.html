CASE FILE / BURRILLVILLE, RHODE ISLAND

The Perron Family Farmhouse

RESIDENCY VERIFIED / PARANORMAL CLAIMS UNPROVEN

The family’s residence and the Warrens’ visits are documented. The witch narrative attached to Bathsheba Sherman is not supported by the historical record.

Assessment published September 2026

Verified factThe Perron family lived at the farmhouse from 1971 to 1980, reported disturbing experiences, and received visits from the Warrens.
UnsubstantiatedApparitions, physical attacks, possession, and demonic activity were not independently established.
Historical correctionNo located record shows Bathsheba Sherman was a witch, murdered a child, or lived in the Perron farmhouse.

The family’s account

Roger and Carolyn Perron moved into the Rhode Island farmhouse with their five daughters in 1971. Family members later described sounds, odors, moving objects, apparitions, and frightening encounters. Their accounts were recorded years later in interviews and Andrea Perron’s books.

Ed and Lorraine Warren visited the home. A séance reportedly upset Carolyn, after which Roger Perron asked the Warrens to leave. The family remained in the house until 1980.

Bathsheba Sherman

The best-known retelling identifies Bathsheba Sherman as a witch responsible for the haunting. Genealogical and town-record research does not support that portrayal. No historical document located by researchers establishes that she practiced witchcraft, murdered a child, hanged herself, or resided in the house.

Attaching unsupported crimes to a real deceased person is not a harmless detail. The paranormal claim and the historical record must remain separate.

Assessment

The family’s residence, testimony, and Warren visits are part of the record. Their cause is unresolved; a supernatural explanation has not been demonstrated. The Bathsheba Sherman story is contradicted by available historical research.
