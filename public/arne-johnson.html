CASE FILE / BROOKFIELD, CONNECTICUT

The “Devil Made Me Do It” Case

HOMICIDE VERIFIED / POSSESSION CLAIM UNPROVEN

Arne Cheyenne Johnson killed Alan Bono in 1981. A judge rejected demonic possession as an evidentiary defense, and a jury convicted Johnson of manslaughter.

Assessment published September 2026

Verified factJohnson fatally stabbed Alan Bono on February 16, 1981, and was convicted of first-degree manslaughter.
Rejected claimThe defense sought to argue demonic possession; the judge ruled that it could not be objectively or scientifically established.
Later disputeMembers of the Glatzel family disagreed about the alleged possession and the Warrens’ account.

The homicide and trial

On February 16, 1981, Johnson stabbed Brookfield kennel operator Alan Bono during a confrontation. The defense announced an unprecedented theory: Johnson had become possessed after challenging an entity allegedly tormenting David Glatzel.

Superior Court Judge Robert Callahan barred that argument, reasoning that possession could not be objectively tested and would be incapable of proof under ordinary rules of evidence. The defense then argued self-defense. On November 24, 1981, a jury convicted Johnson of first-degree manslaughter.

The Warren narrative

The Warrens supported the Glatzels’ belief that David was possessed and said the entity later entered Johnson. Their interpretation connected a period of reported disturbing behavior and religious intervention to the later killing.

That causal link was never established in court. David’s brother Carl later rejected the demonic account and accused the Warrens and others of exploiting the family. Other participants continued to defend it.

What the verdict means

The court did not rule that demons do or do not exist. It ruled that possession was not a legally provable defense and held Johnson responsible under Connecticut law.

Assessment

The killing, attempted defense, ruling, and conviction are documented. Possession remains a disputed religious and paranormal interpretation, not an established cause. The appropriate status is HOMICIDE VERIFIED / POSSESSION CLAIM UNPROVEN.
