HISTORICAL EXPLANATION

FORT DAVIS, TEXAS

The cries of “Indian Emily”

For decades, visitors heard a tragic tale about a young Indigenous woman whose ghostly cries carried through the Davis Mountains. National Park Service researchers later searched military records and found no evidence for the attack or events at the center of the legend.

FindingA documented piece of local folklore, but the historical claim behind it is unsupported.

EVIDENCE REVIEWED

SAN JOSE, CALIFORNIA

Why was the Winchester house never finished?

The famous story says Sarah Winchester built continuously to appease spirits killed by Winchester rifles. The mansion is real and undeniably strange, but historians say direct evidence for séances or a supernatural building order is missing; earthquake damage and repeated remodeling explain many odd features.

FindingThe architecture is verified. The spirit-driven explanation remains an unproven legend.

INCONCLUSIVE

FRESNO & YOSEMITE, CALIFORNIA

The Fresno Nightcrawlers

Grainy security footage appears to show pale, two-legged figures crossing lawns in Fresno and later Yosemite. The clips became an internet-era paranormal legend and drew television and independent investigation, but no definitive identity or authenticated creature has been established.

FindingThe footage exists; what it depicts remains unresolved. Inconclusive is not evidence of an alien being.

PHILADELPHIA, PENNSYLVANIA

Eastern State Penitentiary

Its revolutionary prison design and regime of isolation are extensively documented. Reports of apparitions, voices, and other eerie experiences belong to a separate record of testimony and television investigation—not verified evidence of haunting.

FindingThe prison history is established. Paranormal experiences are reported, but no reviewed source demonstrates a supernatural cause.

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA

The Cecil Hotel

The Cecil’s documented history includes deaths and stays by two serial killers. But an “800 deaths” total is unsupported, and Elisa Lam’s death was officially ruled an accidental drowning—not a paranormal event.

FindingA real history of tragedy has been amplified by unsupported numbers, imprecise retellings, and supernatural speculation.

AMITYVILLE, NEW YORK

The Amityville Horror

Six murders are documented. The Lutz family’s later haunting account became a bestselling book and film franchise, but conflicting versions and missing independent corroboration leave the paranormal claims unproven.

FindingThe crime and cultural phenomenon are verified. The supernatural narrative remains disputed, commercialized, and unsubstantiated.

WESTERN NORTH ATLANTIC

The Bermuda Triangle

Ships and aircraft have been lost in this heavily traveled region, including USS Cyclops and Flight 19. But no official boundary—or evidence of an unusually high disappearance rate—supports the legend of a supernatural zone.

FindingReal losses and some unresolved causes have been combined with distorted accounts and unsupported paranormal explanations.

/ OUR METHOD

From encounter to assessment

  1. Preserve the claim.Record the account, date, location, source, and original media.
  2. Review the evidence.Check files, witnesses, environmental factors, edits, and ordinary explanations.
  3. Label the result.Use clear statuses: Submitted, Evidence Reviewed, Possible Explanation, Unverified, Fact Checked, or Unexplained.
